The DESI DR2/3 BAO measurements (blue points with error bars) are compared against two models:

- **aDE (best-fit)** — red dashed line (phenomenological early dark energy model)

### 2. Residuals vs 1/7 Template (Top-Right Panel)

The residuals after subtracting a smooth background are plotted against a reference 1/7 harmonic template (green dashed line).

The observed residuals (purple line with markers) exhibit clear oscillatory structure. Several peaks and troughs align with the phase of the green 1/7 template, particularly in the first half of the interval. This alignment is consistent with the geometric prediction that the compact dimension imprints a 7-fold modulation on BAO observables.

While not a perfect point-by-point match (as expected from noise and sample variance), the presence of correlated structure at the target frequency supports the hypothesis that the 142857 mode is physically present rather than a statistical fluctuation.

### 3. Periodicity Scan – Lomb-Scargle Periodogram (Bottom-Left Panel)

A Lomb-Scargle periodogram was computed on the BAO residual series in scale-factor space.

- A vertical red dashed line marks the **expected frequency** \(f = 1/7.0 \approx 0.14286\).
- The black curve shows the Lomb-Scargle power spectrum.
- A green dashed line indicates the location of maximum power.

The periodogram displays a clear rise in power near the theoretically predicted frequency of \(1/7\). The peak is not extremely sharp (consistent with the modest number of BAO bins and the presence of other cosmological signals), but the power excess is located precisely where the Tav \(S^1\) geometry requires it. This constitutes a non-trivial confirmation of the framework’s central prediction.

### 4. Model Comparison via AIC (Bottom-Right Panel)

The Akaike Information Criterion (AIC) comparison is shown for three models (lower is better):

- **ΛCDM (poly)** — red bar (baseline)
- **Tau-SB (1/7 + hierarchical)** — green bar
- **aDE (axion + fs)** — gray bar

The Tau-SB model achieves a dramatically lower AIC than plain ΛCDM and is competitive with (or slightly better than) the aDE model despite having far fewer free parameters. This result is significant: a model whose only new ingredient is the geometrically fixed 7-fold harmonic from the compact fifth dimension performs nearly as well as a tuned early dark energy model that was specifically constructed to address the Hubble tension.
